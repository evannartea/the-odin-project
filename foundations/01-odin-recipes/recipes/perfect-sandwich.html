Jake's Perfect Sandwich

Description

The greatest sandwich ever made.

Ingredients

  • Meat
  • Bacon
  • Lobster soul
  • Baguette
  • Cream cheese
  • Pickles (from Prismo)
  • Dill
  • Diced boiled eggs
  • Bird (from the window)
  • Common cucumber
  • Sliced Roma tomato
  • Sweet yellow onions (organic)
  • Carrots
  • Tomatoes
  • Tears (for salt)
  • Rosemary
  • Thyme

Instructions from the realm of creation

  1. Prepare a vegetable stock by bathing carrots, tomatoes, and onions
    in a large pot of boiling water.
  2. Make sure to sharpen your knife using a honing steel six times.
  3. Place your meat in a sealed bag, along with rosemary and thyme.
    Cook it sous vide at 135℉.
  4. Slice your baguette into two halves, then use a blowtorch to toast
    the cut sides of each half.
  5. Spread some cream cheese on each side, before adding sliced pickles
    on one half, and dill on the other half.
  6. Add diced boiled eggs to the side with the pickles, along with
    the bird from the window, sliced common cucumber, sliced Roma tomato,
    sliced sweet yellow onion, tears, and the meat.
  7. Fry the bacon in a pan over high hat, and add this to the sandwich once cooked.
  8. Add the lobster to the boiling vegetable stock and make sure to infuse
    the sandwich with its soul.
